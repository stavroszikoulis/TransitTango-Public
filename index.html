01

Overview

TransiTango ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our iOS and watchOS application handles your information.

TransiTango is designed with user privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or identification markers.

02

Information We Use and How It Is Processed

A. Location Data

  • Usage: TransiTango requests permission to access your device's location to show nearby transit stops, lines, and schedule information on map interfaces.
  • Storage: Your location data is processed exclusively on-device. It is never transmitted to our servers or stored permanently anywhere.

B. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

  • Transit Data APIs: To show real-time transit schedules and updates, TransiTango communicates directly with public transit data providers.
  • No Personal Data Sent: Requests made to transit data endpoints only request schedule or route information and do not include personal user identifiers or location tracking data.
03

Data Storage & Tracking

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register or create an account to use TransiTango.
  • No Third-Party Analytics / Trackers: We do not integrate third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel) or advertising SDKs.
  • No Cookies or Device Fingerprinting: We do not track you across other apps or websites.
04

Children's Privacy

TransiTango does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, children's data is inherently protected.

05

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
